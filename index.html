    About me

    I'm interested & skilled in

    This is Mehedi Hasan, an early-career environmental researcher and development professional with a strong focus on climate action, biodiversity conservation, and environmental policy. I hold a B.Sc. in Environmental Science from Jashore University of Science & Technology and am pursuing the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s in Global Change Ecology and Biodiversity Management (GLOBE), with study periods at Universidad Rey Juan Carlos (Spain), Bangor University (UK), the Universidad Autónoma de Tlaxcala summer school (Mexico) and, since September 2026, the University of Lisbon (Portugal). I am also an intern at the Society for Conservation Biology Europe Region.

    My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of climate adaptation and mitigation, ecosystem-based management, nature-based solutions, and biodiversity conservation, with a growing focus on resilience, sustainability science, and environmental governance. I am particularly interested in understanding human–nature interactions, climate risk and vulnerability, and the role of policy in advancing just and inclusive environmental transitions.

    My work integrates interdisciplinary research, data-driven analysis, and participatory approaches to address complex environmental challenges. I have contributed to programs and research initiatives with organizations such as Oxfam, the RMG Sustainability Council (RSC), the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), the Centre for Environment and Climate Change Research (CECCR), and the Institute of Epidemiology Disease Control and Research (IEDCR), focusing on community-based resource management, climate resilience, and environmental governance.

    I bring technical expertise in GIS and remote sensing, environmental data analysis, and visualization tools such as Power BI, alongside hands-on experience in project implementation and stakeholder engagement. My approach combines spatial analysis, field-based research, and evidence-based policy insights to support sustainable and equitable solutions.

    I am particularly interested in conservation, nature-based solutions, and policy-oriented research, and I am actively seeking opportunities for internships, research collaborations, and future PhD studies. Through my Erasmus Mundus Master’s in Global Change Ecology and Biodiversity Management (GLOBE), I am developing an interdisciplinary perspective on ecosystem dynamics, global environmental change, and biodiversity conservation, which I aim to apply in advancing sustainability, resilience, and inclusive development through impactful, solution-oriented research.

    As an interdisciplinary environmental researcher, I bring experience in biodiversity conservation, climate change adaptation, socio-ecological systems and community-based environmental management. I am skilled in participatory research, stakeholder engagement, environmental governance and mixed-methods research across conservation and sustainability projects. My research interests include environmental justice, ecosystem services, conservation governance, land-use change and sustainable rural development.

    MICROPLASTICS IN INDOOR ENVIRONMENT IN DHAKA, BANGLADESH

    This study investigates atmospheric microplastics in indoor environments in Dhaka, Bangladesh, highlighting their composition, sources, and potential health risks. Using FT-IR, SEM, and EDS, the research identified polyethylene and low-density polyethylene, along with toxic elements like Pb, Zn, and Ni. Findings emphasize the risk of bioaccumulation through inhalation, posing long-term health concerns.
