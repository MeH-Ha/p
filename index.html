    About me

    I'm interested & skilled in

    I grew up in Chandpur, where the Ganges, Brahmaputra and Meghna meet, so I have always seen how closely rivers, land and livelihoods are tied together. That curiosity took me from community fieldwork in Bangladesh to an Erasmus Mundus master’s in global change ecology and biodiversity management, studying in Spain, the UK, Mexico and Portugal.

    What keeps pulling me back to research is one question: when we protect nature, does it really work, and for whom? I want to answer that with careful evidence, and I am looking for a PhD where I can do exactly that.
